Reelsmith is a free video editor that runs in your web browser. It is built for creators, small businesses, teachers and anyone who wants proper editing tools without installing software or uploading their videos.

## What makes it different
- **A real editor, not a template app.** Multi-track timeline, keyframes, masks, color grading, nested sequences and multi-camera editing.
- **Private.** Your media is opened from your own disk and processed on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Free.** No sign-up, no watermark, no time limit.

## How it works
Modern browsers can decode and encode video, run audio processing and use the graphics chip. Reelsmith uses those built-in capabilities to preview your edit in real time and export finished MP4 or WebM files on your device.
