Most online video editors fall into one of two camps. Some are template apps: quick, but you can't really edit. Others upload your footage to a server, make you wait, and add a watermark unless you pay.

Reelsmith takes a different approach. It is a full non-linear editor — the kind of tool editors use every day — that runs entirely in your browser, on your own computer.

## What you get
- **A proper timeline** with unlimited tracks, ripple and rolling edits, slip and slide, the razor tool and frame-accurate trimming.
- **Titles that look designed**: 3D, gradient and two-tone styles, plus word-by-word and letter-by-letter animations.
- **30 transitions**, from a classic cross dissolve to whip pans, glitches and colourful shape wipes.
- **Color grading** with curves, color wheels and scopes.
- **Audio tools**: normalization, EQ, compression, noise gate, voice effects, sound effects and automatic music ducking.
- **Auto captions** in more than 20 languages, generated on your device.
- **Fast export** to MP4 or WebM, with presets for YouTube, Shorts, Reels, TikTok and Instagram.

## Nothing to upload
When you import a video into Reelsmith, it stays where it is on your disk. Your browser reads it, shows it on the timeline and, when you export, writes a new file back to your computer. There is no upload queue, no file-size limit set by a server, and no copy of your footage anywhere else. We explain how this works in [Why your videos never leave your computer](/blog/private-video-editing-in-the-browser/).

## Free, without the catch
There is no account to create and no watermark on your exports. Open [reelsmithapp.com](/) and start editing.
