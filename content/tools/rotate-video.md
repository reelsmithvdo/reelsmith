Filmed sideways or upside down? Rotate the video by 90° or 180°, straighten a slightly tilted shot, or mirror it with a flip — then export a fixed copy.

## How to rotate a video
1. Click **Open the tool** above and import your video.
2. Drag it onto the timeline and select it.
3. In **Effect Controls ▸ Motion**, type **90**, **180** or **−90** in **Rotation**.
4. If the rotated picture no longer fits, raise **Scale** — or switch the sequence to vertical in **Sequence ▸ Sequence Settings…**.
5. Click **Export**.

## How to flip (mirror) a video
Drag **Horizontal Flip** or **Vertical Flip** from **Effects ▸ Video Effects** onto the clip. Horizontal Flip is the usual fix for selfie videos where text appears backwards.

## Straighten a tilted horizon
Small values like **2** or **−1.5** in **Rotation** level a crooked shot. Raise **Scale** slightly to hide the corners.

## Questions
### Can I rotate only part of a video?
Yes. Split the clip with **⌘K** (Ctrl+K) and rotate one piece, or add Rotation keyframes to animate it.
### Will the rotated video keep its quality?
Yes for 90° and 180°. Export with **Match Source – High bitrate**.
