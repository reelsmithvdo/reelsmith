Put a song or background track under your video, fade it in and out, and keep it from drowning out the voice — free, in your browser.

## How to add music to a video
1. Click **Open the tool** above and import your video and your music file (MP3, WAV or M4A).
2. Drag the video onto **V1** and the music onto an empty audio track below it.
3. Drag the end of the music clip to match the video's length, or press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K) to cut it.
4. Fade it: select the music and set **Fade In** and **Fade Out** in **Effect Controls ▸ Audio**.
5. Click **Export**.

## Keep the voice clear
Select the music and apply **Effects ▸ Smart Audio ▸ Auto Duck Music Under Voice**. Reelsmith lowers the music by 14 dB wherever someone speaks, with smooth ramps you can adjust. For a constant level, lower **Volume** instead.

## Use the sound effects library
**Effects ▸ Sound Effects** has 19 original sounds — whoosh, pop, ding, applause and more. Double-click one to add it at the playhead.

> Use music you have the rights to. Platforms like YouTube may mute or claim videos with copyrighted songs.

## Questions
### Can I replace the original sound?
Yes. Unlink the clip with **⌘L** (Ctrl+L), select its audio and press Delete, then add your music. See [remove audio from a video](/tools/remove-audio-from-video/).
### Can I add more than one song?
Yes. Place songs one after another, and drag **Constant Power** from **Effects ▸ Audio Transitions** between them for a crossfade.
