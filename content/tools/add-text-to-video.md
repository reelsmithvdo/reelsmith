Put a headline, a name, a label or a call to action on your video. Type it, style it, animate it and export — free, in your browser.

## How to add text to a video
1. Click **Open the tool** above and import your video.
2. Drag it onto the timeline.
3. Press **T**, click on the video in the Program monitor and type. Click outside the text when you're done — or click **Add title**.
4. Set the font, size, colour, outline and background box in **Effect Controls ▸ Text**.
5. Drag the text clip's edges on the timeline to set how long it stays, then click **Export**.

## Make it stand out
- **Text Styles:** double-click a style in **Effects ▸ Text Styles** — Retro Pop 3D, Neon, Gradient Bold, Sticker Outline and more.
- **Title Templates:** ready-made animated titles such as Lower Third, Chapter Title and Call to Action.
- **Animations:** drag **Words Pop**, **Typewriter** or **Fade Up** from **Effects ▸ Animations** onto the text.
- **Highlight a word:** wrap it in asterisks, like `Make it *pop*`.

> For subtitles from speech, use the [subtitle tool](/tools/add-subtitles-to-video/) instead — it writes and times the text for you.

## Questions
### Can I add text to only part of the video?
Yes. A text clip shows only while it is on the timeline; drag its edges to set the timing.
### Can I add text to a vertical video?
Yes. Click **Safe** in the Program monitor to keep text away from the app's buttons.
