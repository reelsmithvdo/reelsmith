Mute a video completely, silence one part, or replace the sound with music or a voice-over — free, in your browser.

## How to remove audio from a video
1. Click **Open the tool** above and import your video.
2. Drag it onto the timeline.
3. Select the clip and press **⌘L** (Ctrl+L) to unlink the video and audio.
4. Click the audio part (on the A track) and press **Delete**.
5. Click **Export** — the new video has no sound.

## Other ways to mute
- **Mute a whole track:** click **M** on the audio track header. Muted tracks are left out of the export.
- **Silence one part:** split the audio with **⌘K** (Ctrl+K) at the start and end, then delete that piece.
- **Turn it down instead:** lower **Volume** in **Effect Controls ▸ Audio**.

## Replace the sound
After removing the original audio, add music ([add music to a video](/tools/add-music-to-video/)) or record a narration with the **mic** button on an audio track.

## Questions
### Does removing the audio change the picture?
No. The video is exported exactly as before, just without sound.
### Can I remove background noise instead of all the sound?
Yes — see [how to make quiet audio louder and clearer](/tutorials/make-quiet-audio-louder/) for High-Pass, DeHum and Noise Gate.
