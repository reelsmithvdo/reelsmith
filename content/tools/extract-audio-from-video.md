Save the sound of a video as its own file — a song from a clip, a podcast from a recorded call, or dialogue to clean up in an audio app. Reelsmith extracts it on your device, with no upload.

## How to extract audio from a video
1. Click **Open the tool** above and import your video.
2. Drag it onto the timeline.
3. Optional: trim it, or press **I** and **O** to mark the part you need.
4. Choose **File ▸ Export Audio…** (or click **Export Audio** in the guide).
5. Pick **AAC (.m4a)** for a small file that plays everywhere, or **WAV (.wav)** for full quality, then click **Export Audio**.

## Improve it before you export
The export includes every unmuted audio track with its volume, effects and fades, so you can clean the sound first:
- Make it louder and even with **Clip ▸ Normalize Loudness…** — see [how to make quiet audio louder](/tutorials/make-quiet-audio-louder/).
- Remove rumble with **High-Pass** and buzz with **DeHum** from **Effects ▸ Audio Effects**.
- Add music on a second track — both are mixed into the file.

## M4A or WAV?
**M4A (AAC)** is compressed and small, about 1.4 MB per minute, and plays on phones, computers and in browsers. **WAV** is uncompressed (about 11 MB per minute) — best when you will keep editing the sound.

## Questions
### Can I save the audio as MP3?
Reelsmith saves M4A (AAC) and WAV. M4A plays anywhere MP3 does and sounds better at the same size.
### Is my video uploaded?
No. The audio is mixed and encoded in your browser.
### Can I extract audio from several clips at once?
Yes. Put them on the timeline one after another — the export is one file with all of them.
