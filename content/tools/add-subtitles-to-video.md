Add subtitles to any video in your browser. Reelsmith listens to the speech, writes the captions for you and lets you fix, style and export them — free, with no upload and no watermark.

## How to add subtitles to a video
1. Click **Open the tool** above and import your video.
2. Drag it onto the timeline.
3. Open the **Captions** tab and click **Auto-generate**. Choose the language (21 are listed) or **Detect automatically**.
4. Click any caption to correct a word, then click **Style…** to set the size, colour and background box.
5. Click **Export** and choose **Burn into the video**, **Save as an .srt file only**, or both.

## Why use this subtitle generator
- **Private:** speech recognition runs on your computer. Your audio is never uploaded.
- **Accurate when it matters:** pick **Small** for accents and noisy audio, or **Tiny** when you need speed.
- **Translate to English:** tick **Translate the captions into English** for speech in another language.
- **Edit like a pro:** captions sit on their own **CC** track, so you can drag them to fix timing.

> Clean audio gives better captions. Turn music down while generating, or see [how to make quiet audio louder](/tutorials/make-quiet-audio-louder/).

## Burned-in subtitles or an .srt file?
Burned-in subtitles always show and suit TikTok, Reels and Shorts. An .srt file is a separate subtitle track for YouTube or Vimeo that viewers can switch on and off.

## Questions
### Is it free to add subtitles to a video?
Yes — no account, no time limit, no watermark.
### Can I add an existing .srt file instead?
Yes. Choose **Captions ▸ Import Captions (.srt)…**.
### Does it work on a phone?
Yes, in a mobile browser. Generating is slower on phones, so start with the **Tiny** or **Base** model.
