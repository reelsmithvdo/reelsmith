Every platform wants a different shape. Resize your video to **9:16** for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, **1:1** or **4:5** for the Instagram feed, or **16:9** for YouTube — and export at exactly the right size.

## Sizes at a glance
- **TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts:** 1080×1920 (9:16)
- **Instagram square:** 1080×1080 (1:1)
- **Instagram portrait:** 1080×1350 (4:5)
- **YouTube:** 1920×1080 (16:9) or 3840×2160 for 4K

## How to resize a video
1. Click **Open the tool** above.
2. Click **Sequence Settings** and pick a preset, for example **Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920)**.
3. Import your video and drag it onto the timeline.
4. Select the clip and raise **Scale** in **Effect Controls ▸ Motion** until it fills the frame. Drag it in the Program monitor to keep the subject in view.
5. Click **Export** and choose the matching preset, such as **YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok (9:16)**.

## Keep the whole picture
Don't want to cut anything off? Apply **Background Blur Fill** from **Effects ▸ Creative Effects**. The full picture stays visible and the empty space is filled with a blurred copy. See [add a blurred background](/tools/blur-video-background/).

## Questions
### Can I just change the export size?
Yes — the export **Frame size** list has landscape, vertical and square sizes. Changing the sequence first lets you see and frame the result.
### Does resizing add a watermark?
No. Exports are free and never watermarked.
