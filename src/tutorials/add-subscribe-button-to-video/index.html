How to Add an Animated Subscribe Button to Your Video

November 8, 2026 · Tutorials · 3 min read

Add an animated subscribe, like or bell button with a transparent background — and a click-and-bell sound — to your video in a free browser editor.

An animated subscribe button near the start or end of a video is a simple way to grow a channel. A short "subscribe" reminder near the start or end of a video is a simple way to grow a channel. Reelsmith includes animated buttons that are ready to use, with transparent backgrounds — no green screen or background removal needed.

1. Open the Library of subscribe button animations

Click the Library tab (next to Project, Effects and Captions). You'll find:

  • Subscribe Button, Like Button, Comment Button and Notification Bell
  • Like · Comment · Subscribe (all three in one)
  • Follow Button, Share Button, Click Cursor, Bouncing Arrow and Progress Bar

Hover over a card to preview the animation.

2. Add it to the video

  • Double-click a card to add it at the playhead, or
  • drag it onto a track above your video.

It's placed on V2 or higher, sized for your frame — including vertical 9:16 videos.

3. Make it yours

Select the graphic and open Effect Controls ▸ Graphic to change the text (for example SUBSCRIBE → JOIN) and the colours. Move and resize it in the Program monitor, and drag the clip's edges on the timeline to make it stay longer or shorter.

You can also add an In, Out or Loop animation from Effects ▸ Animations — try Pop in and Pulse as a loop.

The click-and-bell sound

The Subscribe Button comes with a matching sound: a click as the cursor presses the button, then a bell as the bell icon swings. The sound is placed on an audio track and linked to the graphic, so they move together.

Don't want the sound? Select the graphic, press ⌘L (Ctrl+L on Windows) to unlink it, then click the audio clip and press Delete.

When to show it

  • After you've delivered something useful — viewers are more likely to subscribe once they've got value.
  • For 3–5 seconds, in a corner that doesn't cover faces or important text.
  • Once or twice per video, not constantly.

Tip: Have your own button animation on a white or green background? See How to remove the background from a video.

Why add a subscribe button?

Many viewers like a video but forget to subscribe. A short, friendly reminder at the right moment turns some of them into subscribers. However, timing matters: a button in the first seconds, before you have delivered anything, is easy to ignore. Instead, show it right after a useful tip or a funny moment, and then once more near the end.

Where to put the subscribe button

  • Bottom third, left or right: clear of faces and of the platform's own buttons. Click Safe in the Program monitor to see the safe zone.
  • Vertical videos: keep it above the bottom quarter, where Shorts and Reels show captions and controls.
  • The last 20 seconds of a YouTube video are usually covered by YouTube's own end screens; show your button before that.

Make it match your channel

Change the colours in Effect Controls ▸ Graphic to your brand colours and the text to your call to action — FOLLOW, JOIN or a word in your viewers' language. Pair it with a title in the same colours; see how to make animated text and 3D titles.

Using your own subscribe animation

Have a subscribe clip on a green, white or black background? Put it on V2 and right-click ▸ Remove Background. The full steps are in how to remove a video background.

Frequently asked questions

Is the subscribe button free to use?

Yes. The Library graphics and sounds are original to Reelsmith and free to use in your videos, including monetized ones.

Does it work for TikTok and Instagram?

Yes. Change the text to FOLLOW and use the Follow Button or Share Button graphics.

How long should the subscribe button stay on screen?

3–5 seconds is enough: long enough to notice, short enough not to annoy.
