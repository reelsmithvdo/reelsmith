How to Make Animated Text and 3D Titles

November 8, 2026 · Tutorials · 3 min read

Create eye-catching titles in a free online video editor — 3D, gradient and two-tone text, word-by-word animation, and ready-made title templates.

A good title grabs attention in the first second. Reelsmith gives you three ways to make animated text for your videos: start from a template, apply a one-click style, or build your own look with text effects and animations. Everything runs in your browser, free.

The fastest way: a title template

Open Effects ▸ Title Templates and double-click one — for example Retro Pop 3D Title, Epic 3D Title, Kinetic Words or Bouncy Words. It's added at the playhead, sized for your frame. Double-click the text in the Program monitor to type your own words.

Other templates cover everyday needs: Lower Third for names, Chapter Title, Countdown 3-2-1, Call to Action and Breaking News Bar.

Type your own title

  1. Press T for the Type tool.
  2. Click in the Program monitor and type. Press ⌘Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows) or click outside to finish.
  3. Drag the text to move it; drag the corner handles to resize.
  4. Set the font, size, colour, outline, shadow and background box in Effect Controls ▸ Text.

One-click styles for animated text

With the title selected, double-click a style in Effects ▸ Text Styles: Retro Pop 3D, Epic Gold 3D, Gradient Bold, Two-Tone, Sticker Outline, Candy 3D, Gold Luxe, Comic Burst 3D, Neon, Elegant and more. Applying a new style replaces the old one, so you can try several quickly.

Build your own 3D or gradient look

Effect Controls ▸ Text Effects has:

  • Gradient fill with two colours and an angle.
  • 3D extrude with depth, direction and side colour.
  • Highlight words: wrap a word in asterisks — Make it *pop* — and it takes the highlight colour.
  • Halftone dots, outer outline, offset shadow, glow and letter spacing.

Add animated text: In, Out and Loop

Drag an animation from Effects ▸ Animations onto the title:

  • In: Pop, Zoom, Slide, Fade Up, Blur In, Bounce, Stamp, Typewriter, and text-only Words Pop, Words Rise, Letters Drop and more.
  • Out: the same moves in reverse, plus Erase.
  • Loop: Pulse, Float, Wobble, Letter Wave or Word Bounce while the title is on screen.

Each title has one In, one Out and one Loop animation. Set their duration, speed and strength in Effect Controls ▸ Animation.

Which animated text style to use

  • Hooks for Shorts and Reels: Words Pop or Bouncy Words — fast and energetic.
  • Vlogs and travel: Fade Up or Typewriter with an Elegant Serif style.
  • Tutorials and interviews: a Lower Third with a gentle Slide In.
  • Calls to action: Pop In plus a Pulse loop so it gets noticed without distracting.

Make animated text match the beat

Titles feel more professional when they appear on a beat or a sound. First, play the music and press M to add a marker on each beat. Then drag the title so it starts on a marker. Finally, add a Pop sound from Effects ▸ Sound Effects at the same point. For example, a three-word hook can pop in word by word on three beats with Words Pop.

Tips for titles that read well

  • Keep it short — three to six words.
  • Use strong contrast: a light fill with a dark outline or shadow is readable on any background. The W3C contrast guideline explains how much contrast text needs.
  • Leave a little space at the edges; click Safe in the Program monitor to see the safe zone.
  • Give viewers time to read: about one second per three words, plus the animation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make animated text without After Effects?

Yes. Title templates, text styles and the In, Out and Loop animations cover the most popular looks with no keyframes needed.

Can I use my own font?

The Font list in Effect Controls shows the fonts available in your browser and on your computer.

Do animated titles work in vertical videos?

Yes. Templates are sized for the current frame, including 9:16. See how to make vertical videos.
