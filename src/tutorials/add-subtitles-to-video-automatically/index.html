How to Add Subtitles to a Video Automatically (Free, Online)

November 8, 2026 · Tutorials · 4 min read

Generate captions from speech in your browser, edit them, and burn them into your video or export an .srt file — free and without uploading your video.

Want to add subtitles to video without typing every word? Reelsmith writes them for you from the speech in your video. The speech recognition runs on your own computer, so the audio is never uploaded — and it is free.

Captions make your videos easier to follow with the sound off, help viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing, and keep people watching longer. The W3C's guide to captions explains why they matter for accessibility.

What you need

  • A video with clear speech (or a separate voice-over track).
  • A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Chrome and Edge are the fastest because they can use your graphics chip.

1. Put the video on the timeline

Open the editor, click New Project (or Start Editing) and drag your video onto the timeline. If the audio is on its own track, that works too — captions are made from everything you can hear.

2. Generate auto captions

  1. Open the Captions tab in the bottom-left panel and click Auto-generate. You can also use Captions ▸ Auto-generate Captions… in the menu bar.
  2. Choose what to transcribe: the Entire sequence, just the part between your In and Out points, or the selected clips.
  3. Pick the spoken language — 21 languages are listed, including English, Spanish, Hindi, Bengali and Arabic — or leave Detect automatically.
  4. Choose the accuracy. Base is a good balance; Tiny is fastest; Small is the most accurate for difficult audio and less common languages.
  5. Click Generate.

The first time, the caption engine downloads once (about 45–95 MB, depending on the model) and stays in your browser. After that it starts almost instantly.

Tip: Tick Translate the captions into English to get English subtitles from speech in another language.

3. Check and fix the automatic subtitles

Speech recognition is good but not perfect — names, brands and technical words often need a correction.

  • Click a caption in the Captions panel and edit its text.
  • Drag caption clips on the CC track to move them, or drag their edges to change how long they stay on screen.
  • Click Add at playhead to add a caption by hand.

4. Style the subtitles

Click Style… in the Captions panel to change the text size, colour, background box, position and margin so the subtitles fit your video. For short vertical videos, larger text with a dark box is easiest to read on a phone.

5. Export with subtitles

Click Export. Under Captions choose:

  • Burn into the video — the subtitles become part of the picture (best for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts).
  • Save as an .srt file only — upload it as a subtitle track on YouTube or Vimeo, where viewers can turn it on and off.
  • Burn in and save an .srt file — both.

You can also use Captions ▸ Export Captions (.srt)… at any time.

Burned-in subtitles or an .srt file?

Burned-in subtitles always show, look the same everywhere and survive re-uploads, which is why they suit short-form apps. An .srt file keeps the picture clean, lets viewers switch subtitles off, and can be indexed by video platforms. When in doubt, choose Burn in and save an .srt file and use whichever the platform needs.

Tips for accurate automatic subtitles

  • Clean the audio first. A High-Pass filter and Normalize Loudness… help the recognizer — see how to make quiet audio louder.
  • Turn the music down while generating, or generate from the voice track only by selecting it.
  • Pick the language instead of auto-detect when a video mixes languages.
  • Use Small for accents, fast speech or noisy places.

How long does it take to add subtitles to a video?

On a recent laptop with the Base model, a one-minute video usually takes well under a minute to transcribe in Chrome or Edge. In addition, the first run includes the one-time model download. As a result, longer videos are best captioned in parts: set In and Out points around each section and choose In to Out.

Correcting the text is usually the slowest part. For example, a five-minute interview might need a dozen fixes for names and brands. However, that is still much faster than typing every line yourself.

Hide captions without deleting them

Untick Show captions in the Captions panel (or click CC in the Program monitor). Hidden captions are left out of exports unless you choose Burn into the video.

Frequently asked questions

Is the automatic subtitle generator really free?

Yes. There is no account, no per-minute limit and no watermark. The speech model is open source and runs in your browser.

Is my audio uploaded to make the captions?

No. The model is downloaded once to your browser and the transcription happens on your device. You can remove it later with Captions ▸ Remove Caption Engine….

Can I add subtitles to a video on my phone?

Yes — the editor works on phones and tablets. Generating captions is slower on a phone than on a computer, so start with the Tiny or Base model.

Can I import an existing .srt file?

Yes. Choose Captions ▸ Import Captions (.srt)… or click Import .srt in the Captions panel, then edit and style them like generated ones. See Captions and subtitles.
