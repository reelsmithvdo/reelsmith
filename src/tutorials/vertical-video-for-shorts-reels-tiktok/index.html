How to Make Vertical Videos for Shorts, Reels and TikTok

November 8, 2026 · Tutorials · 3 min read

Edit 9:16 vertical videos in your browser — turn landscape footage vertical, add captions and text, and export at the right size for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Short-form platforms expect a vertical video in 9:16 at 1080×1920. Here is how to make one in Reelsmith, whether you filmed vertically or horizontally — free, in your browser, with nothing uploaded.

The right vertical video size

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok all play full-screen vertical video. Use 1080×1920 pixels (9:16), keep it under a minute if you are unsure, and export as MP4. YouTube lists its current limits in Upload YouTube Shorts.

If you filmed a vertical video on your phone

Start a project and drop in your phone clip. In a new sequence, the first video you add sets the frame size — a vertical clip makes a 9:16 sequence automatically. A message confirms it, and ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z on Windows) undoes it.

If you filmed horizontally

  1. Choose Sequence ▸ Sequence Settings… and pick the Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) preset.
  2. Add your landscape clip. It sits in the middle with empty space above and below.
  3. Select the clip and open Effect Controls ▸ Motion. Raise Scale until the picture fills the height, then drag it left or right in the Program monitor to frame your subject.

Tip: If your subject moves around, add Position keyframes so the frame follows them. See Effect Controls and keyframes.

Or keep the whole picture with a blurred background

Don't want to crop? Apply Effects ▸ Creative Effects ▸ Background Blur Fill. The full landscape picture stays in the middle and the empty space above and below is filled with a blurred copy — a popular look for reposting horizontal clips.

Make your vertical video work without sound

Most people scroll with the sound off. Therefore, on-screen text matters:

  • Auto captions: Captions ▸ Auto-generate Captions… writes subtitles from your speech — see How to add subtitles automatically.
  • A hook in the first second: press T, click the video and type a short headline. Then try a style from Effects ▸ Text Styles and an animation such as Words Pop from Effects ▸ Animations — In.
  • Keep text in the middle: the top and bottom of the screen are often covered by the app's buttons and captions. Click Safe in the Program monitor to show safe margins.

Keep it moving

  • Cut out pauses with the razor tool (C), then delete the gaps (see Deleting clips and closing gaps) so the video never stalls.
  • Use quick transitions like Whip Pan, Zoom In or Flash between scenes, and the Zoom Punch (beat) effect from Effects ▸ Creative Effects for emphasis.
  • Add a sound effect (Effects ▸ Sound Effects) on key moments.

For more ideas, read 7 editing tips that make short videos easier to watch.

Common vertical video mistakes

  • Text at the very top or bottom. As a result, the app's buttons cover it; keep it in the middle.
  • Black bars above and below. Instead, scale the clip up or use Background Blur Fill.
  • A slow first second. Viewers decide fast, so open on the best moment.
  • Exporting landscape by mistake. Check that the export preset says 9:16 before you click Export.

Export your vertical video at the right size

Click Export and choose the YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok (9:16) preset — 1080×1920 H.264 MP4. If your sequence is already vertical, Match Source gives the same result. Then upload the file from your phone or computer.

Frequently asked questions

What aspect ratio is a vertical video?

9:16 — nine units wide for every sixteen tall. 1080×1920 pixels is the standard size for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Can I make a vertical video from a horizontal one?

Yes. Switch the sequence to the Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) preset, then either scale the clip up to fill the frame or use Background Blur Fill to keep the whole picture.

Can I edit vertical videos on my phone?

Yes. Reelsmith works in mobile browsers with touch gestures, so you can edit and export on the same phone you filmed with.

Will the export have a watermark?

No. Exports are free and never watermarked.
