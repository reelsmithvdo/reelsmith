How to Remove the Background from a Video or Logo

November 8, 2026 · Tutorials · 4 min read

Make a white, black or green background transparent — for logos, subscribe animations and green-screen clips — in a free browser video editor.

Overlay graphics are everywhere, for example: a subscribe animation in the corner, a logo watermark, a green-screen clip of you over a new background. They usually come with a solid background that has to go. Here is how to remove video background colours in Reelsmith in a couple of clicks — free, in your browser.

How it works

Reelsmith removes a background by colour: everything close to the chosen colour becomes transparent, so the video on the track below shows through. This is the same idea as a film studio's green screen, known as chroma keying. It works best when the background is one plain colour that doesn't appear in the subject.

Remove a video background

This works for animations on a white, black or green background, such as a like-and-subscribe clip.

  1. Put your main video on V1.
  2. Put the overlay video on V2, directly above it.
  3. Right-click the overlay clip ▸ Remove Background. (Or select it and click Remove Background in Effect Controls.)

Reelsmith looks at the edges of the frame, detects the background colour and sets up the Ultra Key effect for you.

Fine-tune it

In Effect Controls ▸ Ultra Key:

  • Mode: Brightness for white, black or grey backgrounds; Colour for green or blue screens.
  • Keep inside (edges only): removes only the background that touches the edges of the frame, so white text or icons inside the animation stay visible.
  • Tolerance: raise it if a light fringe remains; lower it if parts of the animation disappear.
  • Softness: smooths the edge.

Place and size it

Use Effect Controls ▸ Motion — or drag the clip in the Program monitor — to scale it down and move it, for example to the bottom of the frame.

Green screen: remove a green background from video

  1. Put the new background (a photo or video) on V1 and your green-screen clip on V2.
  2. Right-click the green-screen clip ▸ Remove Background. Reelsmith picks the green and sets Mode to Colour.
  3. Raise Tolerance until the last green patches disappear, then add a little Softness so hair and edges blend in.

Tip: Light the green screen evenly and stand a step away from it. Shadows and green light bouncing onto the subject are the usual causes of patchy results.

Remove a logo or image background

  1. Import the logo.
  2. Right-click it in the Project panel (or on the timeline) ▸ Remove Background…
  3. The preview shows transparent areas as a checkerboard. Click the preview to pick the background colour, or Shift+click to add more colours for gradients.
  4. Choose Background touching the edges to keep white parts inside the logo, or This colour everywhere to also clear the holes in letters like O, A and e.
  5. Adjust Tolerance and Edge softness, tick Trim transparent edges if you like, and click Remove Background.

A new transparent PNG is added to the Project panel and your clip switches to it. The original stays untouched.

Fixing common problems when you remove a video background

  • A thin white or green outline stays: raise Tolerance a little, then add Softness.
  • Parts of the subject vanish: lower Tolerance, or tick Keep inside (edges only) so only the outer background is removed.
  • The background is a gradient: for images, Shift+click several colours in the preview.
  • The background changes colour in the video: colour removal needs a steady colour; use a mask instead (below).

Remove video background for a logo watermark

A transparent logo in a corner makes your videos look consistent. First, remove the logo's background as shown above. Then place it on the top video track for the whole length of the video, scale it to about 10–15 % of the frame width, and lower it in Effect Controls ▸ Opacity so it doesn't distract. Finally, check it against both light and dark scenes.

When colour removal isn't enough

Background removal works by colour, so it's best for graphics and screens with a plain background. To cut out a person in an everyday photo or video, draw a mask around them and use mask tracking to follow the movement.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove a video background without a green screen?

Yes, if the background is a plain colour such as white or black. For busy, real-world backgrounds, use a mask with tracking instead.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The effect runs on your computer's graphics chip while you edit and export.

Does the transparent part export as transparent?

The transparent area shows the tracks below it, and the exported MP4 contains the combined picture. MP4 itself has no transparency.
