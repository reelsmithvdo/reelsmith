Compress Video Online

Compress video online for free: make a smaller MP4 for email or chat with a lower resolution or quality setting. Nothing is uploaded — it all runs locally.

Open the tool — free

Make a video small enough to email, share in a chat or upload faster. Reelsmith compresses on your own computer, so even big files never leave your device.

How to compress a video

  1. Click Open the tool above and import your video.
  2. Drag it onto the timeline.
  3. Click Export and choose the Review copy (small file) preset — 540p at a lower bitrate.
  4. Need more control? Choose Custom, then lower the Frame size (for example 1280×720) and set Quality to a lower level.
  5. Click Export.

What makes a file smaller

  • Resolution: 720p files are much smaller than 1080p or 4K, and still look sharp on phones.
  • Quality (bitrate): a lower quality setting saves the most space; fine for talking-head videos.
  • Length: trim the start and end first — see trim a video.
  • Format: VP9 (.webm) is often smaller than H.264 at the same quality, but MP4 plays everywhere.
Compress once, from the original. Re-compressing an already small file loses detail each time.

Questions

Is compressing private?

Yes. The file is processed in your browser; there is no upload, so it is also faster than online services for large files.

How small will my file be?

It depends on length and settings. A minute of 540p review copy is usually a few megabytes.
