Trim Video Online

Trim video online for free: cut the start and end, remove parts from the middle and export MP4 — in your browser, with no upload and no watermark.

Open the tool — free

Trim a video to the part you want in seconds. Reelsmith is a full editor in your browser, so you can cut the start and end, remove pieces from the middle and export a new MP4 — without uploading anything.

How to trim a video

  1. Click Open the tool above and import your video.
  2. Drag it onto the timeline.
  3. Trim the start or end: move the mouse to the edge of the clip until the cursor changes, then drag inwards.
  4. Remove a part in the middle: press C for the razor tool and click at the start and end of the part. Press V to go back to the selection tool, click the piece and press ⇧Delete (Ripple Delete) so the gap closes.
  5. Click Export.

Trim precisely

  • Zoom the timeline with the + and − buttons (or = and −) to work frame by frame.
  • Press ← / → to step one frame, then ⌘K (Ctrl+K) to split at the playhead.
  • Want only one section? Press I and O to set In and Out points, then choose Range ▸ Sequence In/Out when you export.
Trimming in Reelsmith never changes your original file. You can undo any cut with ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z).

Questions

Will trimming reduce the quality?

The export is re-encoded. Choose Match Source – High bitrate to keep it as close to the original as possible.

Is there a file size limit?

No server limit — files open from your own computer, so the only limit is your device.

Can I trim a video on my phone?

Yes. Drag the clip edges with your finger, and long-press for the clip menu.
